station-nas-drive

個人自用工具：由使用者自己的 NAS 定期核對自己 Google Drive 備份資料夾內的舊備份副本， 確認 NAS 已完整保存後，將超過保留期的副本移至 Google Drive 垃圾桶。

A personal, single-user tool that runs on the owner's own NAS. It verifies old backup copies in the owner's own Google Drive backup folders against copies stored on the NAS, then moves expired copies to the Drive trash. It is not offered to other users.
