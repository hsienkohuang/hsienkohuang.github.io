隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

本工具僅供擁有者本人使用，只存取擁有者自己 Google Drive 中指定的備份資料夾：列出檔案、讀取檔案內容以計算 SHA-256 校驗值， 並將已在擁有者 NAS 上驗證完整保存的過期副本移至垃圾桶。

資料只在擁有者自己的 NAS 上處理；不分享、不出售、不傳送給任何第三方，不用於廣告或訓練任何模型。 授權憑證只保存在擁有者的 NAS，擁有者可隨時於 Google 帳戶「第三方存取權」撤銷授權。

This tool is used only by its owner and accesses only designated backup folders in the owner's own Google Drive (listing files, reading content to compute SHA-256 checksums, and moving verified expired copies to the trash). Data is processed only on the owner's own NAS and is never shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, nor used for advertising or model training. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party access settings.
